# Roadmap

## 1. Roots mapping (`roots/`)

Map **all modern-day behaviours** to the three roots — greed, hatred, delusion — treated as entities/patterns governing behaviour, not just momentary feelings.

- Each root gets a profile: how it feeds, what it demands, what strengthens it.
- Modern behaviours filed under roots: doomscrolling, over-ordering, performing, numbing, …
- Visual language: entity visuals (e.g. green greed entity).

## 2. Advanced mode: Abhidhamma

Full Abhidhamma layer underneath the simple roots view — cetasikas, process,
conditionality — for those who want precision. Simple by default, Abhidhamma
one level down.

## 3. AI chat that actually helps

A chat harness (simple pi harness + fojin MCP over the canon) that:

- helps a diarist **define their behaviours** precisely (which root? what pattern?),
- grounds guidance in both the canon and the repo's real journey corpus,
- improves as the database of journeys grows.

## 4. Community journeys

Other people's diaries + tensions accumulate. The corpus of real attempts
becomes the training context no textbook can replace.
