# Greed — lobha (the green entity)

Canon: "Lobho akusalamūlaṁ" — greed is a root of the unskillful (AN 3.69).
Whatever the greedy one plans by body, speech, or mind is unskillful, and
"many bad unwholesome states are born of greed, have greed as source"
(lobhajā lobhanidānā lobhasamudayā lobhapaccayā aneke pāpakā akusalā dhammā
sambhavanti).

## Entity profile (user's language)

- **Feeds on:** delight. Enjoying the cake strengthens it.
- **Demands:** more next time. Whole slice today → the pull toward more than
  one tomorrow. Greed = anything that makes you order more than 1 next time.
- **Punishes unfed-ness:** restlessness, the morning phone-pull, the itch.
- **Weakens when:** unfed repeatedly (javana without repetition → āsevana
  fades), or replaced (three-quarters slice, shared chunk, flapjack, banana,
  nothing, stop showing up — the cake ladder from day-01 notes).

## Abhidhamma (advanced)

- 8 lobha-mūla cittas: feeling (pleasant / neutral) × prompted / unprompted ×
  with / without wrong view. Wrong view here = the cake-will-fix-it belief.
- Always co-arising: moha (delusion is present in *every* unwholesome citta),
  plus view, conceit, restlessness depending on class.
- Javana: 7 impulsion moments per sense-door process; each conditions the next
  by repetition (āsevana-paccaya). Delight-now literally builds stronger
  pull-later. Upton's "Unwholesome Impulsion" table tabulates this.

## Seed observations (day-01)

- goodbye-coffee + "fed the wolf" logic → lobha, with moha (rationalization)
- extra cake slice / autopilot eating → lobha
- morning phone-pull → lobha
- making dharma content instead of earning → candidate lobha (delight in the

## Antidote root

Alobha (non-greed): generosity, renunciation, the cake-ladder in reverse —
contentment with less until absence is easeful.
