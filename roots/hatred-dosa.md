# Hatred — dosa (aversion)

Canon: "Doso akusalamūlaṁ" — hate is a root of the unskillful (AN 3.69).
Same machinery as greed: whatever the hating one plans by body, speech, or
mind is unskillful; many bad states are born of hate, have hate as source.

## Entity profile (user's language)

- **Feeds on:** pushing away. Not just rage — irritation at the café server,
  dread of the feeling, the flinch from shame.
- **Demands:** avoidance, armouring, numbing. Pays for protection with
  unconscious energy (the shame-armouring budget from day-01).
- **Punishes unfed-ness:** the feeling floods back the moment the armour
  drops — which is why the armour feels necessary.
- **Weakens when:** the pushed-away thing is allowed to be present without
  reaction. Same coals logic: water (struggle) makes steam; leaving alone
  lets them cool.

## Abhidhamma (advanced)

- 2 dosa-mūla cittas: both with aversion (paṭigha); prompted or unprompted.
  Always co-arising: moha + aversion + (envy, avarice, worry, in classes).
- Note: shame-avoidance reads as dosa at the citta level even when the story
  is about identity — the mechanism is aversion to felt experience.

## Seed observations (day-01)

- shame-armouring around hidden sexuality → dosa (aversion to feeling) +
  moha (wrong view of self that must be defended)
- coffee resistance-struggle itself ("I shouldn't but I will") → candidate
  dosa overlay on a lobha base — fighting the pull feeds steam

## Antidote root

Adosa (non-hatred): loving-kindness, acceptance, letting the feeling sit
unfed. Armour laid down deliberately, in safety, bit by bit.
