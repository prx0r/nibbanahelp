# Cetasika decode — experience → what's in the mind

The Visuddhimagga/Sangaha set of 52 mental factors, each rendered in
experience language ("what it feels like when it's present"), for decoding
moments — not labelling situations. Source backbone: Abhidhammattha Saṅgaha
ch.2 (Bodhi); Pa Auk/Upton tables at bethupton.com/resources-and-tables
(52 Mental Factors, Unwholesome Impulsion, Wholesome Impulsion PDFs —
exact co-arising sets live there). Tensions carry `factors:` in their
header; the vis lights what's active.

## Law of co-arising (what rides together)

Every unwholesome moment carries the 4 universal unwholesomes:
**moha, ahirika, anottappa, uddhacca** — delusion, shamelessness, moral
fearlessness, restlessness. They are never absent from akusala. Each root
class then adds its own: greed-class adds lobha (+diṭṭhi and/or māna
variants), hate-class adds dosa, doubt-class moha-mūla adds vicikicchā.
The exact javana (impulsion) sets: Upton's Unwholesome Impulsion table.
Seven impulsion moments run per process, each feeding the next by
repetition (āsevana) — that's why decoding a moment matters: you're
reading the set that's currently being fed.

## The 14 unwholesome — decode table

4 universal (in every akusala moment):

| factor | feels like | our example |
|---|---|---|
| moha | fog; can't see the state AS a state; story feels like reality | "I need the coffee, period" — taken as fact, not mind |
| ahirika | no inner wince at the action; the shame arrives later or never | ordering the third cup without flinch |
| anottappa | no fear of the consequence; consequences feel abstract | "tomorrow will handle it" |
| uddhacca | scattered, can't settle; the churn | 3am money-thoughts, jumping between worries |

3 greed-class (with lobha-rooted moments):

| factor | feels like | our example |
|---|---|---|
| lobha | pull toward; the lean-in; anticipation | the pull toward the second coffee |
| diṭṭhi | the belief riding the pull: "this will fix it" | "the cup = relief" — wrong view of the object |
| māna | self-image as the one who needs/appears a certain way | flinch comparing to earners; armouring |

4 hate-class (with dosa-rooted moments):

| factor | feels like | our example |
|---|---|---|
| dosa | push away; irritation; the flinch from a feeling | hating the debt-pressure itself |
| issā | the comparing burn: they have, I don't | comparison to people without debt |
| macchariya | hoarding-instinct; guardedness with what's held | clutching the little time/money |
| kukkucca | worry-loop; remorse-playback; "what have I done" | the debt re-litigation, coffee regret |

3 others (with moha-rooted moments):

| factor | feels like | our example |
|---|---|---|
| thīna | heavy-lidded stall; can't start | avoiding opening the ledger |
| middha | numb-tired; the body refuses | doomscroll instead of practice |
| vicikicchā | the doubting stammer: earn or practice? | the core of the debt knot |

## The 25 beautiful — what grows instead

19 universal beautifuls (saddhā → paññindriya: faith, sati, hiri, ottappa,
alobha, adosa, tatramajjhattatā + the 12 purity factors), 3 abstinences
(sammā-vācā/kammanta/ājīva), 2 illimitables (karuṇā, muditā), 1 wisdom
faculty (paññindriya/non-delusion). Decode side: the antidotes are not
installed, they're what's present when the unwholesome isn't being fed —
the cake-ladder's other side. Active-growth tracking lives in the vis
under `factors_growing:` on tensions.

## How to use (the decoder)

1. In experience: name the pull/push/fog precisely → check this table →
   the moment's citta-class is whichever root's factors are present,
   always with the 4 universal riders.
2. Chat probing asks for factors, not roots (already in CHAT_SYSTEM —
   this table is the vocabulary).
3. Vis: `factors:` on a tension = the decode of its latest state;
   `factors_growing:` = beautifuls being fed. The 14-grid lights up.
4. Corrections replace, never delete — the arc from crude to precise is
   the record (see debt → moha/dosa correction).
