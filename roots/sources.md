# Sources behind the graph

## Canon (verified verbatim via fojin)

- **AN 3.69 Akusalamūlasutta** — three unskillful roots (lobho, doso, moho);
  whatever one plans by body/speech/mind under a root is unskillful; many bad
  states born of each root; the tree-strangled-by-creepers simile; the three
  wholesome roots (alobho, adoso, amoho); root-cutting → parinibbāna in this
  very life.
- **Iti 50 ("Roots")** — "there are these three unskillful roots: greed,
  hate, and delusion."
- **AN 10.174** — deeds of body/speech/mind threefold by greed, hate, or
  delusion (killing, stealing, harsh speech, covetousness, ill will…).
