# Delusion — moha (the fog)

Canon: "Moho akusalamūlaṁ" — delusion is a root of the unskillful (AN 3.69).
Same formula: whatever the deluded one plans by body, speech, or mind is
unskillful; many bad states are born of delusion.

## Entity profile (user's language)

- **Feeds on:** not-seeing. Autopilot cake-eating, the "tomorrow will be
  easier" story, the belief that the fix is out there (money, cake, coffee).
- **Demands:** nothing — that is its trick. It operates by cover, not by pull.
  You don't feel moha; you feel its children (the craving, the dread).
- **Present everywhere:** in Abhidhamma, moha co-arises in *all twelve*
  unwholesome cittas. Every greed has fog in it; every hatred has fog in it.
  Always check for moha.
- **Weakens when:** seeing clearly — "the anything that does catch attention
  is only temporary, self-liberating" (day-01). Wisdom is moha starved of
  cover.

## Abhidhamma (advanced)

- 2 moha-mūla cittas: one with doubt (vicikicchā), one with restlessness
  (uddhacca). The doubting citta is the "is this even the path?" mind; the
  restless one is scattered fog.
- Doubt specifically blocks the path — it cannot coexist with the clarity
  that discerns mentality/materiality (Upton's Pa Auk discernment frame).

## Seed observations (day-01)

- "fed the wolf today, tomorrow easier" junky-logic → moha rationalizing lobha
- "nibbana requires emptying the whole hard drive" overwhelm → candidate moha
  (doubt-class: path looks impossible, so why start)
- cake-will-solve-it belief → moha (wrong view inside lobha-citta)

## Antidote root

Amoha (non-delusion, wisdom): seeing the temporary as temporary. Discernment
practice — mentality and materiality distinguished, not mushed (Upton).
