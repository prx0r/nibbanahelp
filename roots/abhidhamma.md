# Advanced mode: Abhidhamma (Beth Upton's discernment)

The roots graph is crude on purpose — training wheels. Its failure mode:
labeling a whole *situation* ("debt") with one root ("greed"). A life
situation is not a citta. It is thousands of mind-moments, each with its
own root and its own crew of mental factors. Advanced mode stops asking
"which root is this situation?" and asks Upton's question instead:
**what exactly is arising, moment by moment?**

## The 12 unwholesome cittas

- **8 lobha-mūla** (greed-rooted): pleasant or neutral feeling × prompted
  or unprompted × with or without wrong view. The wrong-view ones carry
  the belief ("this will fix it"); the viewless ones are pure pull.
- **2 dosa-mūla** (hate-rooted): always with aversion (paṭigha) and
  painful feeling; prompted or unprompted.
- **2 moha-mūla** (delusion-rooted): one with **doubt** (vicikicchā —
  "earn or practice? is this even the path?"), one with **restlessness**
  (uddhacca — scattered, can't settle).

## The 14 unwholesome cetasikas (who rides along)

Four are universal — present in *every* unwholesome moment: **moha**
(delusion), **ahirika** (shamelessness), **anottappa** (fearlessness of
wrongdoing), **uddhacca** (restlessness). Then: lobha, **diṭṭhi** (wrong
view), **māna** (conceit — "I must appear a certain way"), dosa, envy,
avarice, **kukkucca** (worry/remorse — including money-worry),
sloth-torpor, and doubt.

So the debt knot, discerned rather than labelled: doubt-cittas
("dharma now or earn now?"), worry/restlessness about money (kukkucca,
uddhacca), aversion to the pressure (dosa moments), conceit flaring when
comparing to earners (māna). **Lobha barely features.** The old
"debt = greed" label was wrong — corrected 2026-10-29. The coffee knot:
lobha-mūla cittas (delight/anticipation, some with the wrong view that
relief lives in the cup) + moha's rationalization crew. The shame knot:
dosa-mūla (aversion to felt experience) + māna (the armour is conceit
defending an image) + moha (wrong view of self).

## Javana (already in the graph, now precise)

Seven impulsion moments per sense-door process; each conditions the next
by repetition (āsevana). Upton's "Unwholesome Impulsion" table maps exactly
which cetasikas co-arise in each javana round. Feeding the entity =
strengthening āsevana; leaving alone = letting it fade. Same mechanics,
now with names for every passenger.

## Upton's discernment method

From the Pa Auk lineage: first discern materiality, then mentality —
breaking experience into ultimate realities until the meditator sees each
citta with its factors directly, not as "my debt problem" but as:
doubt-moment, worry-moment, aversion-moment, each self-liberating on
seeing. Her tables (52 mental factors, 34 beautiful factors, wholesome /
unwholesome impulsion: bethupton.com/resources-and-tables) are the field
guides. The AI's probing questions should hunt **factors**, not roots:
restlessness? doubt? view? conceit? worry? — then name the citta-class.

## Circumstance vs affliction (locked)

The debt is real. The mental affliction it incurs is something else
entirely. Never conflate them:
- **Circumstance** (debt, bills, body, weather): to be handled
  practically. Right action here is ordinary and honourable — figure
  out a way to make money and sustain oneself (Right Livelihood,
  AN 4.62's debtlessness as lay happiness).
- **Affliction** (doubt, worry, aversion, conceit about the
  circumstance): to be discerned, not obeyed and not "solved".
  This is where the path happens — inside the circumstance, never
  after it.

Mixing them up produces both errors at once: spiritual bypassing
("I shouldn't care about money, I'm practicing") and worldly
drowning ("I can't practice until I'm solvent"). The split resolves
both: handle the money, practice the mind, same day.
