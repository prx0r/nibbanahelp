# Antidotes

Canon: "Alobho kusalamūlaṁ, adoso kusalamūlaṁ, amoho kusalamūlaṁ" — non-greed,
non-hatred, non-delusion are the roots of the skillful (AN 3.69). Whatever the
un-greedy one plans by body, speech, or mind is skillful. Root-cut → "in this
very life he attains parinibbāna" (diṭṭheva dhamme parinibbāyati) — nibbana
now, not later.

## 1. Non-feeding (leave the coals)

Pouring water (struggle, suppression, willpower war) makes hot steam — more
suffering, warmer coals. Leaving alone lets them cool: each unfed javana round
weakens āsevana (repetition condition). Method: don't watch closely, don't
attend, just leave it alone (day-01). Applies to all three roots.

## 2. Replacement (the cake ladder)

Greed is a complex psyche-pattern; abrupt abandonment disturbs. Swap the
harmful for the less harmful that still satisfies the ritual:

whole slice → three-quarters → shared chunk → half → flapjack → banana →
nothing → stop showing up (wasn't hungry).

Same shape for coffee (coffee now → tea next → ?), phone mornings
(resist the pull daily → peace in a month), any lobha pattern.

## 3. The three wholesome roots (grown, not installed)

- **Alobha:** generosity, contentment with less. Grown by giving the chunk away.
- **Adosa:** letting the feeling sit unfed; armour down in safety.
- **Amoha:** seeing the temporary as temporary; discernment (Upton's
  mentality/materiality frame).

Upton's practical frame: kusala = happy mind states; design your life to get
more kusala — beautiful qualities co-arise in a kusala moment the way
unwholesome factors co-arise in javana (10 Happier ep; her impulsion tables).
