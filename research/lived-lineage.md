# Lived lineage: attainment diaries + daily-life teachers

Research for nibbanahelp — who has already done the "honest diary / honest
Q&A" thing, and what we steal from each. Searched 2026-10-28.

## 1. Attainment diaries (first-hand chronologies)

### Ñāṇavīra Thera — Letters (the model)
- 150 letters from a kuti in Bundala, 1960–65, many answering readers'
  questions about the Notes — i.e. exactly our diary+tensions+advice shape.
- He **announced his own sotāpatti in a letter** (mid-1964, see letters index).
  That is a "day X of attaining nibbana" entry in the wild.
- Full text: https://nanavira.org (letters by year, post-sotapatti section,
  Sister Vajirā correspondence) + free PDF:
  https://www.dhammatalks.net/Books7/Nanavira_Thera-Clearing_the_Path.pdf
- Other languages: translated into Czech, Dutch, German, Sinhalese,
  Serbo-Croatian (Notes only).
- Living continuation: **Hillside Hermitage** (Ven. Ñāṇamoli) — one video per
  Note of Notes on Dhamma + Q&A, books *Dhamma Within Reach*, *The Only Way
  to Jhāna*: https://www.hillsidehermitage.org
- Steal: the epistolary form. Dated letters answering real confusion =
  our entries schema with zero changes.

### Pragmatic dharma practice logs (the corpus precedent)
- **Dharma Overground**: thousands of dated public practitioner diaries,
  2000s→today: https://www.dharmaoverground.org
- **Daniel Ingram, MCTB** (free: https://www.mctb.org) — claims stream entry
  mid-90s, lays out the 16-ñāṇa maps; the "maps vs territory" debate that
  followed is itself data on how diaries get misread.
- **Kenneth Folk** — "first and last training", systematic stages, KFD forum
  archive (1,400+ topics): https://awakenetwork.org/forum/kfd-root
- **johnduality.net/practice-logs** — someone already scraped DhO+KFD logs
  into a spreadsheet **labelled with Claude**, remixable via GitHub. Proof
  our "AI reads the diary corpus" loop is buildable, and prior art to cite.
- Steal: public-by-default logs + community diagnosis. Our tensions format
  is a stricter version of a DhO log header.

### Thai forest life-stories (attainment as narrative)
- **Mae Chee Kaew** — village woman → enlightenment, via the great masters
  of her era; free bio: https://forestdhamma.org/ebooks/english/pdf/Mae_Chee_Kaew.pdf
- **Ajaan Lee autobiography** (Thai → Thanissaro): forest adventures as
  teaching vehicle: https://www.dhammatalks.org/books/AutobioAjaanLee/
- Steal: liberation stories where debt/shame/householder-life appear as plot,
  not footnotes. Our diaries are these, in real time.

### Post-awakening diaries (what it's like after)
- **Bernadette Roberts, *The Experience of No-Self*** — a 2-year diary after
  the self dropped; then *What Is Self?*. Free PDFs on archive.org.
  Christian vocabulary, same territory: the falling-away recorded day by day.
- **Suzanne Segal, *Collision with the Infinite*** — 8 years living with no
  personal self, including the fear. The "what do I do on Tuesday" record.
- Steal: they prove the diary doesn't end at the event. Our day-01 → day-N
  arc needs their "years 1–8" shape as the long horizon.

## 2. Daily-situation Q&A teachers (the hidden GOATs)

### Ajahn Jayasaro (the English Thai monk)
- Hermit at Khao Yai since 2003; books in Thai/English + **Chinese, French,
  Italian, Portuguese**: https://www.jayasaro.panyaprateep.org/en/book
- Daily reflections feed: https://www.jayasaro.panyaprateep.org/en/reflection
- Author of *Stillness Flowing* (Ajahn Chah biography) — knows the whole
  lineage from inside.
- Steal: the reflection-a-day form. Short, daily, practical. Our entries
  template could grow a "Jayasaro-style reflection" prompt.

### Abhayagiri Q&A archive (the advice corpus)
- Every question tagged, clustered, with audio: the **everyday-life cluster**
  is our tensions list asked live — time speeding up off-retreat, samādhi
  absent in daily sits, anatta at work, refuge in awareness mid-day:
  https://www.abhayagiri.org/questions/clusters/everyday-life-relevant.html
- Teachers: Pasanno, Amaro, Chah (via archive), Jayasaro tag included.
- Steal: the cluster format. Our future advice engine wants exactly this:
  situation → teacher → audio/text, tagged by root.

### Sayadaw U Tejaniya (daily-life awareness as the method)
- Ex-businessman, householder till 1996 — lived both worlds, teaches
  awareness *in* market/work/laundry, not apart from it.
- Books from yogi interviews (= our tensions+advice shape):
  *Don't Look Down on the Defilements* (13 languages), *Awareness Alone Is
  Not Enough* (practice-discussion transcripts), *When Awareness Becomes
  Natural* (everyday-life manual). Plus a **Chinese-only** collection.
  https://ashintejaniya.org/teachings
- Key lines for our graph: investigate the defilement rather than making it
  disappear; wisdom gets its chance when defilement subsides (Sept 2025
  quotes). That is our javana/antidote mechanics in a teacher's voice.
- Steal: his interview method — first contemplate the defilement itself,
  then return to the object. That is a tension-processing protocol we can
  encode.

### Ajahn Chah (the source voice on daily practice)
- *A Still Forest Pool* (Kornfield ed.) — Q&A form, dhutanga/everyday
  mindfulness. Free PDF: https://hongtaoh.com/files/cnblog/A_Still_Forest_Pool.pdf
- *Questions and Answers with Ajahn Chah*: https://ajahnchah.org/book/Questions_Answers_with_Ajah.php
- His method: "examine your own mind-states, interview yourself" — the
  diarist as their own teacher. That sentence belongs in our CONTRIBUTING.
- Steal: self-interview as practice. Our entries template already does this;
  name him as the reason.

### Ajaan Fuang ("Awareness Itself") + Ajaan Lee
- Fuang taught Thanissaro for 10 years; *Awareness Itself* is short,
  daily-life-adjacent instruction: https://accesstoinsight.org/lib/thai/fuang/itself.html
- Steal: brevity. Fuang's answers are often one paragraph. Our AI advice
  should default to one paragraph, Fuang-style, with expansion on demand.
