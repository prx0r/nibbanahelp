# Daily-experience voices: people figuring it out + teachers on the mundane

Second sweep (2026-10-28): blogs and books where the struggle is daily-life
sized — cravings, phones, procrastination, work — and teachers who explain
*doing it* well. The answers are in the suttas; the hard part is Tuesday.

## 1. Judson Brewer — the clinical mirror of the cake ladder

Addiction psychiatrist + Buddhist teacher (drjud.com). Habit loop:
trigger → behavior → reward, for anything from morning coffee to
phone-checking to anxiety itself.

- Smoking study: a participant paid close attention to what smoking
  *actually tasted like* for the first time — "once she paid attention, the
  spell broke." That is amoha dissolving a lobha loop by direct seeing,
  measured in a lab. Our javana/āsevana mechanics, in RCT form.
- First Gear practice: Trigger → Behavior → Result, written down. One loop.
  "Not a simplified version or a preview. That's the actual practice."
  This is a tension log with three columns — compatible with our format.
- Apps for craving/smoking/overeating/anxiety exist, but the books carry the
  method without the product.
- Relay use: Brewer is the bridge voice for skeptics — same mechanism as
  the roots graph, zero Pali required.

## 2. Noah Levine / Recovery Dharma — the junky-logic lineage

- *Dharma Punx* (memoir): raw addict diary → practice. The "I'm a junky so
  that's what I'll do" honesty, published.
- Talk **'Just One More'** (Against the Stream, July 2021): the exact voice
  of the goodbye-coffee / one-more-slice mind, from a teacher.
  https://www.againstthestream.com/dharma-talk-and-meditation-podcast/just-one-more
- **Recovery Dharma** (free book + inquiry workbook): Buddhist recovery for
  *process* addictions — tech, food, shopping, sex, work, worrying — with
  First/Second Truth inventory worksheets. A structured tension inventory
  that predates ours; study it before finalizing `schema/tension.md` v2.
  https://recoverydharma.org (book PDF free via NYC chapter site)
- Note: Levine faced misconduct allegations (2018, ATS centers closed).
  Use the material, don't platform the man. Flag: teachers are data, not
  gurus — the relay cites words, never endorses persons.

## 3. r/streamentry — the living daily corpus

- Rule 1: top-line posts must be personal practice (incl. off-cushion sīla).
  Weekly practice-update threads = thousands of dated micro-diaries.
- Long reports (e.g. "500 hours of daily meditation in my first year")
  show the full arc including doubt and backsliding.
- Relay use: link-first, Reddit ToS respected. Best source for
  "someone struggling with my exact thing this week".

## 4. Tejaniya specifics (teacher explaining the *doing*)

From Sept 2025 quotes + *Awareness Alone Is Not Enough* (yogi-interview
transcripts — the book IS daily Q&A):

- "If defilement arises, first contemplate the defilement itself. When it
  subsides, bring the object back to mind and look at it again."
- "Meditation is not yet finished just because the defilement has
  disappeared. We should continue to investigate."
- "Is it a superficial knowing? Or is it a clear and precise knowing?"
- Three wisdoms: heard → thought → directly experienced. Our tension arc
  (open → loosening → resolved) is the third one accumulating.
- ClassicalTheravada thread collecting daily-life bhavana quotes from
  contemporary teachers:
  https://classicaltheravada.org/t/bhavana-development-in-daily-life-quotes-from-contemporary-teachers/2135

## 5. Michael Taft — Deconstructing Yourself (bridge voice)

35+ years across Zen/Tantra, ex-Sounds True editorial director. Blog +
podcast, practice-first, allergy to jargon. "Taking practice from the
cushion into everyday life" is an explicit beat. Nondual frame — useful
where Theravada vocabulary repels. https://deconstructingyourself.com
