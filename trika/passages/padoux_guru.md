# Padoux — "The Tantric Guru" (Tantra in Practice ch.1) · extract

> Source: R2 `atlas-sources` → local `corpus/incoming/tantra-in-practice.epub` (ch.1).
> Role in deitybody: source text for the "grace at the limit" lock (AGENTS.md) — dīkṣā and the guru's function, stated by Padoux rather than asserted by us.

## Core points

- The guru carries "a real, active, all-powerful and grace-bestowing presence of the deity existing in, or rather as, the guru. He has to be divine because in spiritual matters one gives not so much what one has as what one is."
- Worship opens with **gurusmaraṇa** — invocation of the guru by which the practitioner "places him/herself mentally within his/her own tradition." It is the *succession* (guruparamparā / gurupaṅkti) that is made present, not the individual: in Kaula lines a threefold flow (ogha) — divine, accomplished, human — linking every teacher back to the revealing godhead.
- Function: the guru is the grace-delivery point and the tradition-placement point. Practice without placement is free-floating — which is exactly what our provenance tags (SOURCE_ATTESTED, recension labels) do textually.
