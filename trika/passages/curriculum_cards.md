# CURRICULUM CARDS — how to perform each stage

> Companion to `docs/CURRICULUM.md` + `data/curriculum.json`. Each card: DO / IN THE MIND /
> NOT / SOURCE. ASSEMBLY for wording; SOURCE lines are the attested anchors. Governing order
> (MV 2.21, six-stage syllabus): **sound + articulation + touch + locus FIRST; visualization
> (dhyāna) only after the association is stable.** Touch ladder: external touch →
> proprioceptive → imagined → effortless locus → glyph.

---

## 1 · Full body round
- **How to hold the sound (phonetics, not doctrine):** the clips are pronunciation *models* — hear the shape, then your own voice holds it. **Vowels ride your exhale** (MV 2.21 uccāra; phoneme shines in rising breath — Padoux), length set by comfort, never forced (TĀ 4.91). **Relative length is fixed by Sanskrit svarabhakti/śikṣā: short = 1 mātrā, long = 2** — i.e. ā ī ū ṝ ḹ e ai o au (and aṃ/aḥ) held roughly DOUBLE a i u ṛ ḷ. **Consonants are contact + release, never held** — aspiration is release, not duration; nasals/semivowels short; h may breathe through briefly. Never stretch a recording to make it longer (vowel-quantity rule).
- **DO:** Seat, natural breath. For each of 50: chant aloud, touch its place, look at the glyph — three at once. One sound complete before the next. Gap 4–15s.
- **IN THE MIND:** the triad: sound arriving + touch + place known. No image yet — you are building the association the image will later ride on.
- **NOT:** no visualization (dhyāna comes later), no forced breath ratios (TĀ 4.91), no zoning out — the mind rides each sound to its place.
- **SOURCE:** TĀ 15 install ("the whole body… transformed into mātṛkā"), MV 2.21 five components, touch ladder.

## 2 · Vowel powers (PTv nights)
- **DO:** The guided night: frame → directed rounds → open loop. Chant at the locus holding the power-word with the sound.
- **IN THE MIND:** *a* is consciousness, *ā* bliss, *i* will — the power as meaning held while chanting, not imagined as color or light.
- **NOT:** rare vowels (ṝ/ḷ/ḹ) = touch + breath, silence, never substitute; no invented imagery for powers the recension doesn't describe.
- **SOURCE:** PTv vowel table (Singh recension), TĀ 15 loci.

## 3 · Varga isolation
- **DO:** One limb, five slow rounds: chant along the arc as you touch it, shoulder → fingers.
- **IN THE MIND:** five points fused into ONE continuous arc — the shape of the limb, kinesthetic. Slower than the full round: coverage isn't the aim, the arc is.
- **NOT:** no stopping at each point as separate stickers; don't add visualization yet.
- **SOURCE:** TĀ 15 varga loci (ka = shoulder→arm→hand→fingers→nails, verse-literal), MV 2.21 karaṇa.

## 4 · Recall both directions
- **DO:** Quiz, no chart. Sound plays → let the point arrive *before* answering. Point named → let the sound name itself. ~20 answers, then stop.
- **IN THE MIND:** the flash of knowing-before-looking. This is the stage where the map stops being external — retrieval, not reading.
- **NOT:** don't guess fast; the pause before the answer IS the training. Stop at the bar (40+/50), don't grind.
- **SOURCE:** smṛti as encoding (Flood), anusamdhāna as criterion (Padoux).

## 5 · Reverse pass
- **DO:** Full 50 from kṣa toward a, slower than install; touch optional.
- **IN THE MIND:** subtraction — the field thins as you go; each sound leaves rather than fills. The direction asks: what remains when it empties? Nothing to hold.
- **NOT:** no skipping the rare sounds to make it smooth; no turning it into speed — saṃhāra is deliberate consumption.
- **SOURCE:** kṣa→a liberation direction (Padoux ṣaḍadhvan); saṃhāra/sṛṣṭi two-direction practice (six-stage syllabus).

## 6 · Aṅga five points
- **DO:** Heart, head, shoulders, eyes, hands — formula voiced aloud at each place, three rounds. Formulas from the TĀ-15 extract.
- **IN THE MIND:** one gesture of the five: whole body, one sweep. Speed with clarity — under a minute is the result of clarity, not haste.
- **NOT:** if a formula is unsure, slow that round; correctness first, the 60s bar second. (Score unbuilt — read formulas from `corpus/extracts/TA15_NYASA.md`.)
- **SOURCE:** TĀ 15 aṅga formulas (aṅ kaṁ khaṁ ghaṁ ṅaṁ āṁ hr̥dayāya namaḥ …).

## 7 · Mantra trajectories
- **DO:** One trajectory looped with a breathing gap. Chant along if the voice is ready; listen if not. Light follows the recorded sound.
- **IN THE MIND:** attention rides the route — each syllable landing on its place. Sound first; the light is confirmation, not the object.
- **NOT:** don't watch the screen instead of hearing; don't stack two mantras in one sitting.
- **SOURCE:** mantra = sonic body of the deity (Flood/Khanna), OM spine (Padoux 422–425).

## 8 · Ascent + nectar
- **DO:** OM loop, station by station heart → dvādaśānta, one breath per station; then let the descent happen.
- **IN THE MIND:** ride up, then release the riding. The nectar is not a second task — it is what happens when you stop climbing.
- **NOT:** no literal anatomy (Padoux: the anatomy is imaginal); no forcing the descent or timing it.
- **SOURCE:** OM 11-stage spine (Padoux 422–425) + Netra amṛta down-flood.

## 9 · Mālinī rewrite (gated)
- **DO:** Only after Mātṛkā is automatic: the same 50 in her order na→pha, at her loci.
- **IN THE MIND:** active unlearning — when a sound lands somewhere new, let it. The strangeness IS the content.
- **NOT:** never relabel Mātṛkā dots as Mālinī; never "correct" a Mālinī position back to Mātṛkā; never mix geometries in one sitting.
- **SOURCE:** MV 3.37–41 / TĀ 15.121–125 order; Mālinī = real second config at madhyamā (Padoux 341–342).

## 10 · Gaze inward
- **DO:** Vision on one point, body wide around it, breath untouched; let outer sound turn inward.
- **IN THE MIND:** mouth may soften or stop; the sound continues internally (vaikhari → madhyamā). Wander → return to the point, not to effort.
- **NOT:** no strain-gazing; no judging the session by how still the mind was — the gate is the ascent available on demand, not a stillness score.
- **SOURCE:** VBT dhāraṇā (Lakshmanjoo reference; v24 score provenance), levels of speech (Padoux ch.4).
