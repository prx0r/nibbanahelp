# PTv vowel powers per locus (Parātrīśikāvivaraṇa)

Source: deitybody site/data/ptv_nights.json — restatement of Jaideva Singh's recension vowel table; loci per TĀ 15 (verse-literal). Ṛ/ḷ/ḹ carry no gloss in the table — held in silence.

## Night 1 — Night 1 — a · ā (ground + bliss)
- **a** (अ) at forehead: cit — consciousness itself, anuttara, the unsurpassable ground
- **ā** (आ) at mouth: ānanda — bliss, the ground enjoying itself

## Night 2 — Night 2 — i · ī (will + mastery)
- **i** (इ) at right eye: icchā — will to manifest, still untouched by objectivity
- **ī** (ई) at left eye: īśāna — mastery; will now coloured by objectivity

## Night 3 — Night 3 — u · ū (knowing + waning)
- **u** (उ) at right ear: unmeṣa — jñānaśakti, the power of knowledge opening
- **ū** (ऊ) at left ear: ūnatā — waning of knowledge, whence object-appearance begins

## Night 4 — Night 4 — ṛ · ṝ (imperishable)
- **ṛ** (ऋ) at right nostril: amṛtabīja — the imperishable seed, subject to no change
- **ṝ** (ॠ) at left nostril: held in the amṛta field — no separate gloss in this recension; breathe, do not substitute

## Night 5 — Night 5 — ḷ · ḹ (void-pause)
- **ḷ** (ऌ) at right cheek: śūnya-pause — binds nothing; rest at the locus, no sound
- **ḹ** (ॡ) at left cheek: śūnya-pause — binds nothing; rest at the locus, no sound

## Night 6 — Night 6 — e · ai (action indistinct + distinct)
- **e** (ए) at lower teeth: asphuṭa kriyāśakti — indistinct power of activity
- **ai** (ऐ) at upper teeth: sphuṭa kriyāśakti — distinct power of activity

## Night 7 — Night 7 — o · au (action clearer + clearest)
- **o** (ओ) at lower lip: sphuṭatara kriyāśakti — more distinct power of activity
- **au** (औ) at upper lip: sphuṭatama kriyāśakti — most distinct power of activity

## Night 8 — Night 8 — aṃ · aḥ (contraction + emission)
- **aṃ** (अं) at crown (śikhā): Śiva-bindu — undivided knowledge of the universe, the holder
- **aḥ** (अः) at tongue: visarga — emission itself; lower dot Śakti (world expands), upper dot Śiva (all rests in I-consciousness)
