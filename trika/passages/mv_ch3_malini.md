## Why MV ch.3 matters for deitybody

Abhinavagupta treats the *Mālinīvijayottara* as essential Trika scripture. Chapter 3 is where Mālinī-as-alphabet becomes **operative nyāsa** for the Śakti-body.

---

## Key stanza 36 (bhinna-yoni / śākta-śarīra)

```text
Viśeṣavidhihīneṣu nyāsakarmasu mantravit |
Nyasecchāktaśarīrārthaṁ bhinnayoniṁ tu mālinīm ||36||
```

**Sense:** With respect to nyāsa acts that have no special procedure, the mantra-knowing one should perform nyāsa of **Mālinī** — **bhinna-yoni** (vowels and consonants mixed) — **for the sake of the Body of Śakti (śākta-śarīra)**.

Pradīpaka gloss (purpose of nyāsa):

> Nyāsa is placing the hands on different parts of the body while repeating mantras… goal: identification with particular deities, or full identification of macrocosm and microcosm… **the final goal is the Śakti aspect (I-consciousness)** — the root of all mantras is Śakti, this root is Aham.

aṅganyāsa purpose:

> transformation of the gross physical body into a divine reality by means of assigning sacred sounds to different parts of such physical body.

---

## Bīja / yoni = Śiva / Śakti (st. 10–12)

- **vowels = bīja (seed) = Śiva**
- **consonants = yoni (womb) = Śakti**
- Mātṛkā keeps them in two ordered blocks
- **Mālinī mixes them** → bhinna-yoni

---

## Contrast of orders (Pradīpaka commentary under st. 36)

**Mātṛkā (ordered):**

```text
a ā i ī u ū ṛ ṝ ḷ ḹ e ai o au aṃ aḥ
ka kha ga gha ṅa ca cha ja jha ña ṭa ṭha ḍa ḍha ṇa
ta tha da dha na pa pha ba bha ma ya ra la va śa ṣa sa ha kṣa
```

**Mālinī (mixed, nādiphāntā na→pha):**

```text
na ṛ ṝ ḷ ḹ tha ca dha ī ṇa u ū ba
ka kha ga gha ṅa i a va bha ya ḍa ḍha ṭha jha ña ja ra ṭa
pa cha la ā sa aḥ ha ṣa kṣa ma śa aṃ ta e ai o au da pha
```

## Mantra extraction note (after st. 41)

Chapter then **draws out** mantras by citing body-assigned letters rather than letters directly. Example: Aparā mantra **Hrīḥ hūṃ phaṭ**; Parā essentially **saḥ**; Parāparā long Oṃ…phaṭ formula.

For deitybody Month 1–2: **install the alphabet; do not chase mantra extraction yet.**
