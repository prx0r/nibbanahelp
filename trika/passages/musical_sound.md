## 1. Vowels ARE music (TĀ 3.183–184)

`svara` = both vowel-sound and musical note. Vowels are "the musical sound
(svara), as it were, of consonants" (`svaramayaṃ`); consonants live because
"vowels are their very life-breath" (`svaraprāṇaṃ`).
— vol2 ~13994/22141, G:1759–62.

**Implication: melody attaches to VOWELS. Consonants are onsets, vowels carry
the tone. Our tone-per-locus is directionally right; durations must follow
vowel quantity (ā/ī/ū/ṝ/e/ai/o/au long).**

## 2. Sweetness hierarchy (TĀ 3.237–245)

| Level | Sound | Character |
|---|---|---|
| gross Paśyantī | ālāpa: notes, no words | sweet, undivided |
| gross Madhyamā | drum (mṛdaṅga): half-distinct | still sweet |
| gross Vaikharī | distinct letters, sentences | harsh (paruṣa) |

"Undifferentiated unity is called power/Śakti" (`mādhuryaṃ śaktir ucyate`,
3.238); divided-into-letters is harsh. Middle pleases "insofar as undivided"
(3.242–243, proved by tāla-pāṭha delight in unmanifest resonance).

**Implication: ascent = SIMPLIFICATION. Higher stops get LONGER, FEWER notes
— melody dissolves toward unity, never busier. Drum-layer (pulses) is
correctly madhyamā-grade.**

## 3. Music → identity, instantly (TĀ 3.239–240, 3.209–210)

Immersed in musical sound, "experiences in an instant oneness" (`tanmayībhūtir
jhagity eva`) "by shared nature" with consciousness. Sweet song → Heart
vibration = bliss-power (VBh 73, IP 1.5.14). Collective string-sound absorbed
as ONE (not separate notes) = samādhi door. "We become the music."

**Implication: add the DRONE — collective sustained sound under trajectories
(Sa+Pa tanpura bed). The door is unity, not sequence.**

## 4. Gandharva path + Sarasvatī built of music theory

Vaikharī-notes → established on madhyamā/paśyantī (SvT via Kṣemarāja).
Seven notes (ṣaḍja…gāndhāra…, Vedic accents origin, emotion-communication).
Sarasvatī's body: three grāmas navel-lines, seven-note body, tāna hair,
mūrchanā horripilation, tāla feet, gīta texts seat (SvT 10/837–8).
Vīṇā microtone connoisseur = instant nondual knowledge (MVV 1.180–2).
Dull man unmoved by music = jaḍa.

## 5. Song/dance are ritual-obligatory, not decorative

Pavitraka festival "of song and dance" (28.140); collective song-gathering =
shared bliss, Śāmbhavopāya (28.375–6); vīra-cakra with vīṇās+flutes;
Kaula heroes DEFINED as song-instrument lovers (29.95–97);
mudrā → song/dance/cakra-expansion (JY). Vīra = one who sings.
