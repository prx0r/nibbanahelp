# Flood — "The Purification of the Body" (Tantra in Practice ch.29) · extract

> Source: R2 `atlas-sources` → local `corpus/incoming/tantra-in-practice.epub` (ch.29). Fullest source: Jayākhya-saṃhitā (Pāñcarātra, 7th–10th c.), parallel to the Śaiva Tantras/Āgamas.
> Role in deitybody: the step BEFORE nyāsa — our Phase 1 opens at seat+frame with no purification. This chapter says what a traditional opening does.

## Sequence (Jayākhya pattern)

```text
bath / aspersion (snāna)
  → purification of the elements of the body (bhūtaśuddhi — dissolve tattvas back into source)
    → divinisation through nyāsa (touch + mantra)
      → mental / internal worship
        → external worship
```

Purification "overcodes the vedic body with the tantric cosmology" — the old body is ritually unmade (elements dissolved) so the mantra-body can be installed. Nyāsa without bhūtaśuddhi is installation onto an unprepared surface.
