# Day 1 of attaining nibbana

## Current tensions

### Debt

I am in debt, 1500 pounds, yet instead of focusing on making money I am making dharma content. Why? Because nibbana is only attained now.

### Coffee

I am addicted to coffee. I said I wasn't going to order it, but now it's getting to it and I'm pretty sure I'm going to order it. I am going to also order tea and then switch to that instead of the coffee. I am going to order the coffee, as a final goodbye. I think this is the beautiful part, I know I shouldn't do it, I'm going to do it, tomorrow will be easier to quit because I have fed the wolf today. That's my logic right now, it's like how a junky justifies, but I am a junky, so that's what I'm going to do.

### Shame

I am bisexual but not openly. I'm sure a lot of my unconscious energy is spent protecting myself from feeling the shame of this and feeling like I need to act a certain way. This is why nibbana is so tough I think, because necessarily it means you must confront the entire psyche — nibbana is the complete emptying of the hard drive.
